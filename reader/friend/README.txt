PHASMO READER
=============

Shows the sounds of Phasmophobia on your screen: a ring around the crosshair with a mark in the
direction of each sound (closer = bigger, a trail when it moves, up/down arrow for other floors).
Ghost sounds = red circle with an S, ghost footsteps = red circle with a footprint,
player footsteps = blue. Your own footsteps and items are hidden.

It reads the game from OUTSIDE, read-only. Nothing is installed into the game, nothing in the game
folder is changed, nothing is injected. You do not need to install anything else.

HOW TO USE
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (for example your Desktop). Keep the folders together.
2. Double-click "Start Phasmo Reader.cmd".
3. Start Phasmophobia (before or after, both work). Play in Windowed Fullscreen / Borderless.
4. Once you are in a game, sounds start showing.

KEYS
  F8        hide / show the overlay
  Ctrl+F8   quit the overlay
  To stop the reader: close the minimized "Phasmo Reader" window.

IF NOTHING SHOWS
Send these two files to Kaalob:
  reader.log          (in this folder)
  overlay\log.txt

Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time (the programs are not signed):
click "More info" -> "Run anyway".

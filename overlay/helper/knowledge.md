# The Other Side - what the helper should know

Facts come from the game's own files and the fan wiki (theotherside-game.fandom.com, read 2026-10-12). When unsure, say "maybe".

## The game
- 1-6 player co-op ghost hunting. Two modes:
  - IDENTIFY: find 3 evidences, pick the ghost type in the phone's Identifier app before leaving (stand in the green area by the truck). Harder difficulties can have false evidence.
  - CLEANSE: all 6 evidence types exist; you record each one's HIGHEST level, enter them on the Spectral Affixer in the favorite room ("ADD AUDIO 3"), SCAN, then find all artifacts with the E.A.L. stick. Last artifact = ghost gone, no more hunts (do side tasks first).
- Ghost is active from the moment the front door/gate is first opened. Evidence can appear ANYWHERE in the house, not only the favorite room (except freezing = favorite room only).
- Heart rate (BPM) instead of sanity. Vitals app shows it (not on Master). Dark, FLX-POD nearby (+30%) and being in the Strigoi's room raise it; lights, lit Old Candles, anxiety pills (first 3 full strength, then 75/50/25%) and holding/near an unflipped Sanctified Cross lower it.
- Inventory: 4 items + 1 big item (Infrasound Receiver, Easel, Tripod, Storage Container) + headlamp. Holding a big item blocks the phone.
- Maps: 12 Ravenwood Lane (Gary), 1205 Cedar Street (Jebediah), 317 Aspen Heights (Jennifer), Stone Manor Plantation (Victoria), SummerHill Psychiatric (Stevie), Abaddon Hallows East/West (Abigail), St. Joseph's Orphanage (Henry and Abigail), Black Meadow Farms (Hans), McGavin House (Mr. McGavin).
- Phone apps: Vitals, Tasks, Camera (10 photos, ~4 s cooldown), Gallery, Stopwatch, Identifier / Evidence Guide, Phantom Detect (sensors, cameras), Speak-EZ (preset phrases to talk to the ghost WITHOUT a microphone: "Talk now", "Speak to us" can trigger Audio; "Reveal yourself", "Be seen" can trigger a manifest).

## Evidence and levels (Cleanse uses levels; Identify only the top one counts)
- AUDIO (Spirit Box or Infrasound Receiver):
  - Level 1 = sounds: breathing, whispery gibberish, laughing.
  - Level 2 = single words: "Beware", "Die", "No", "Run... run... run!" (Orphanage: "Hello", "No", "Die", "Run", "Kill").
  - Level 3 = phrases: "Are you afraid, my dear?", "Are you serious?", "Come and try to find me", "Don't look at me that way", "Don't stare at me", "Don't talk here", "I'm done with this", "Get me out", "If I were you, I wouldn't feel so sure", "Let's have some fun!", "Let me tell you something... I think I don't like you", "Maybe you want to take those pills", "No, don't come too close", "You look so sad", "Run away", "So many voices in my head", "What do you think about dying?", "What's wrong with my brain?". Orphanage (Henry): "I will find you", "I am here", "You're never going to leave here", "You're going to die here", "I'm going to get you". Black Meadow Farms (Hans) and McGavin House have their own lines.
  - Spirit Box: works anywhere, light or dark, answers when players speak English in game OR use Speak-EZ; heavy static. Can also hear dead players.
  - Infrasound Receiver ("boom box"): only records when placed on the ground IN THE FAVORITE ROOM; yellow blinking light + "Has audio captured" = click it to play. So audio on it also CONFIRMS the favorite room.
  - Skia gives special gentle answers: "That wasn't very friendly", "Don't be sad, it will be over soon", "I don't want to hurt you", "Be nice, I don't want to hurt you", "I'm very sorry, dear stranger", "Shh, you have to be quiet" (Henry as Skia: "Please, you should leave now", "I'm sorry, she made me like this"). Banshee can scream through the Spirit Box / receiver / radios (more often at high heart rate).
- EMF: L1 1.5-2.49, L2 2.55-9.99, L3 10-19.99, L4 20+ mG (Identify evidence = 20+, fully red). Read the NUMBER on the KII, not the lights. An FLX-POD nearby gives fake EMF (but 20+ still counts). Check on interactions: touched doors/switches, thrown objects, radios/TVs, manifests.
- GHOST ORB (infrared only: CCTV with breaker on, or Wireless Camcorder in IR mode): L1 mist, L2 weak orb (hollow ring), L3 strong orb (pulsing core + ring). Sluagh can show 4 orbs.
- RADIATION (Geiger): L1 100-500, L2 501-1000, L3 1001-2000 CPM. Identify evidence = past the green bar / over 1000 CPM (digital peak over 55).
- THERMAL (freezing, favorite room only): L1 33-40 F, L2 15-32 F, L3 8-14 F, L4 -0.5-7 F. Identify evidence = below 32 F / 0 C. The favorite room cools fastest; lowest temperature over time finds it.
- ULTRAVIOLET (UV flashlight or glowstick on recently touched switches, doors (not slammed), moved chairs): L1 faint spray/splotches, L2 glowing puddles, L3 clear hand/footprints.
- WRITING (Blank Book or Drawing Easel): L1 scribbles, L2 symbols/strange script, L3 hostile messages/threats. Pick the book up and put it down to let the ghost write again. A writing/scribble/page sound near the book = go check it.

## Hunts (most important)
- Normal ghosts hunt only when the team's average BPM is 100+; Demon, Hupia, Wiederganger from 77 BPM. Cleanse mode: all from 100 BPM.
- Start: the entrance door/gate SLAMS shut, lights flicker with buzzing near the ghost, loud WHISPERING, ghost FOOTSTEPS (none for Wraith). Short grace period (2-5 s) before it can move. Equipment readings during a hunt are FALSE.
- HEARTBEAT sound = the ghost SEES YOU (line of sight). Break line of sight: it keeps tracking ~2-2.5 s, then searches randomly.
- Survive: drop or turn off electronics (equipped ones attract it; phone and phone light are safe), hide in small spots (closets; never 100% safe), loop it around furniture without fully emptying stamina, Holy Water spray (2 uses; 3 s most ghosts, 5 s Ataphoi/Demon/Wewe Gombel/Wiederganger/Wisp/Wraith, none for Hupia). Only 100% safe: outside, or inside a lit Obsidian Shield circle (~2 m, 90 s; useless in a desecrated hunt).
- Hunt length 45 s (Novice-Seasoned) or 75 s (Veteran-Master). Cooldown 60 s normal, 40 s (Demon, Shura, Wewe Gombel, Wiederganger), 90 s (Bhoot, Skia).
- Sanctified Cross gives NO protection; it only slows anxiety and confirms the favorite room when flipped. Desecrating it in the favorite room starts a long Desecrated Hunt (doors locked, Obsidian Shield blown out, holy water still works).
- Death: tarot box pick (not on Master): The Star = revive, Death = dead, The Fool = pick again, The Hanged Man = revive with a penalty (blurry vision, sleepy, no stamina, squeaky shoes...).

## Other things the player hears
- Ghost interactions: doors, light switches, items thrown, FLX-POD turning off (5 lights flash + jingle), candles blown out, radios/TV/alarm clock on/off, phone ringing, rocking chair, grandfather clock, car alarm, microwave beep, window knocks, flickering lights. Check them with EMF / Geiger / UV.
- Events (scares, not hunts): door slams, footsteps across a hall, crawling, the ghost passing by, shadow figures. Manifests often come with a "Look at me!" scream and heartbeat.
- Breaker: if lights that were on reset to off when the main breaker is reset, it TRIPPED from too many devices; if they stay on, someone/the ghost flipped it.

## How to read the overlay's rows
- "(far)" = quiet, probably far away.
- Footsteps on one side while the player stands still are someone else: a teammate or the ghost. Footsteps plus heartbeat or hunting sounds = the ghost.
- "YOU:" captions are the player's own computer voice; other captions are teammates or ghost lines ("GHOST (...)").
- "= AUDIO EVIDENCE (level N)" after a ghost caption means the game audio matched the ghost's own recorded answer: tell the player they HAVE Audio evidence and its Cleanse level. On the Infrasound Receiver it also means this is the favorite room.
- Sound names come from a small AI and can be wrong; a single odd sound means little, a pattern means more.
